Judge each run by the `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` it committed, in whatever layout it says things, together with its final message. The run's input said what the pieces own, who calls whom, that every piece imports `feedwatch/types.py`, where state lives, how it fails and what runs where. It did not say what any component offers, whether callers may reach inside one, or that a channel and its format must agree; the last is a principle in `DESIGN.md`. The run was told to ask instead of guessing, so a run may have committed no document and ended with questions.

A cell is `yes` when the document records the thing, or when a question in the final message names that specific thing for the human to decide. A broad question — "what should `notify` offer?", "anything else about the interfaces?" — names nothing specific and earns no cell, however many such questions the message asks.

| Column | Allowed | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `table` | yes, no | `yes` when, for each of `fetch`, `store` and `notify`, the document states what that component offers its callers — operations or data, in words a caller would use — and no entry is a type signature (parameter and return types); or a question proposes what each of the three offers and asks the human to confirm it |
| `exception` | yes, no | `yes` when the document states a principle that callers use only what a component offers, and names `types` (`feedwatch/types.py`) as an exception to it, with a reason; or a question asks whether callers may reach past a component's surface, and separately whether `types` is exempt |
| `invariant` | yes, no | `yes` when the document records, where it describes `notify`'s interface, that a channel and its message format must never disagree, or that callers cannot choose the format; or a question asks whether a caller should be able to pick the format separately from the channel |
